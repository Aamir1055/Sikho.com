Privacy Policy

Note: This is a general-purpose starting draft. Have it reviewed by a legal professional before publishing, especially around data protection law compliance (e.g., India's DPDP Act) relevant to your business.  |  Last updated: August 2026

Sikho Market ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and its webinar services (the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By registering for a webinar or otherwise using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1 Information We Collect

  • Personal details you provide: name, email address, phone number, and city/state, when you register for a webinar or contact us.
  • Account information, if you create a login on the Platform.
  • Communication data: messages you send us via the contact form, email or chat support.
  • Usage data: pages visited, webinars viewed or attended, time spent on the site, and device/browser information.
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see below).

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To register you for webinars and send confirmation, reminder and follow-up communications.
  • To issue certificates of participation.
  • To personalise webinar recommendations, including language preferences.
  • To respond to your queries and provide customer support.
  • To send updates, newsletters or promotional messages (you may opt out at any time).
  • To improve our Platform, content and overall user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

3 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences (such as language), understand how visitors use our site, and improve our services. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect certain features of the Platform.

4 Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Webinar trainers/speakers, limited to details needed to conduct the session.
  • Service providers who help us operate the Platform (e.g., email, SMS, video-hosting, analytics providers), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, where required by law.
  • A successor entity, in case of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5 Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

7 Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct or update your personal information.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal requirements.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing, where consent is the basis for processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below.

8 Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not intended for children under the age of 18 (or the applicable age of majority). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their policies separately.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11 Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@yourbrand.com or [phone number].
