Quick answers

Before you write

These cover the questions we hear most often.

How do I book a free webinar seat?

Open the home page, choose your language, and tap Book Free Seat on the session you want. Tamil sessions go to a dedicated booking page; other languages can be confirmed through this form if a booking page is not yet live.

When will I get a reply?

Support is available Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 6:30 PM IST. Most messages get a response within one business day. Messages sent on Sunday are handled the next working day.

Can I join in my own language?

Yes. Sessions run in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Gujarati. Mention your preferred language on the form so we can reply in the one you are most comfortable with.

How do I apply to become a trainer?

Select Become a trainer as the subject, tell us your topic and language, and include a short note about your experience. Our partnerships team will follow up if there is a fit.

I have a technical issue during a live session.

Choose Technical issue and include the webinar name, time, and what you saw on screen. If you are in a live session, mention that in the first line so we can prioritise it.
